title: What the West Can Hold
description: A personal reflection on queer Western identity, inherited grit, and making tradition roomy enough for truth.

publishedAt: 2026-08-18
readingTime: 6 min read
---

I was raised around the idea that your word matters, work should be done right, and neighbors show up when they are needed. Those lessons still belong to me.

So does the truth that I am a gay cowboy. For a long time, those parts of a life were treated like they had to stand on opposite sides of a fence. I no longer accept the fence.

The West is not one story. It is labor and migration, loss and reinvention, family we inherit and family we choose. Tradition is strongest when it can tell the truth about who has always been here.

Sage & Spur Ranch is a place to make that truth visible without turning a life into a slogan. There is room for boots worn by work, tenderness without apology, and a wider gate.
